# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not the person who wrote
it.

## What is expected

- Keep technical disagreements technical. "This is wrong because X" is welcome; "this is wrong
  because you don't understand X" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. A project stays coherent by declining things, including
  reasonable things.
- Be patient with people who are new to Rust, to TypeScript, or to the codebase. Everyone was.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or unwelcome attention of any kind. Publishing
someone's private information. Deliberately derailing discussions.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers privately. Reports are read by the maintainers only, and are
handled discreetly.
